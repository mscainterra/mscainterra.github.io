An Integrated Framework for Climate-Resilient Earthen Cultural Heritage

INTERRA investigates how earthen cultural heritage responds to changing environmental conditions, connecting material behaviour, field monitoring, numerical modelling and digital integration to support climate-resilient conservation.

About the project

From material response to heritage resilience

Earthen heritage is highly sensitive to moisture, temperature and environmental change. INTERRA develops an integrated research framework to better understand these interactions and translate them into practical knowledge for conservation and risk assessment.

The project combines experimental characterisation, in-situ monitoring, heat-air-moisture modelling, climate scenarios and digital heritage tools across multiple case studies and research environments.

Research

Four connected research objectives

INTERRA moves from the material scale to building and heritage-site applications, linking measurements, models and digital information.

01

Materials

Hygrothermal characterisation and modelling of earthen materials and assemblies.

02

Monitoring

Environmental and material monitoring across heritage sites exposed to different climates.

03

Climate & modelling

Heat-air-moisture simulations and climate scenarios for assessing future performance and risk.

04

Digital integration

Integration of monitoring, modelling and heritage information within digital environments.
